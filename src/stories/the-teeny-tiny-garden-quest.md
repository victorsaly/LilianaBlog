## Scene 1 — The Sparkly Mushroom
> Draw this: the sunny back garden. Lili kneeling by a tiny glowing mushroom, with Leo, Mum, Dad, Pip and Blaze close by.

It was a sunny Saturday, and the family was out in the garden.

Lili was poking about in the grass when she found something strange. A tiny mushroom — and it was glowing.
![Lili](/art/illustrations/lily.png)

"Wow, look at this!" she called. "It's all sparkly!"

Leo came over for a look. "Don't touch weird glowing things, Lili," he said. Then, of course, he touched it.
![Leo](/art/illustrations/leo.png)

The mushroom went *PFFT* and puffed out a cloud of silver dust. It floated over the whole family.
![a tiny glowing mushroom puffing out a sparkly silver cloud of dust over a surprised family in a sunny back garden](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-1-illo-1.png)

"Achoo!" sneezed Mum.
![Mum](/art/illustrations/mum.png)

"Bless you," said Dad. "What a funny little—"
![Dad](/art/illustrations/dad.png)

He never finished. Because right then, everything began to grow. The flowers grew taller. The grass grew taller. The fence grew enormous!

"Um," said Lili. "I don't think the garden is getting bigger..."

## Scene 2 — Teeny Tiny Trouble
> Draw this: the whole family shrunk down to the size of ladybirds, standing under towering blades of grass like a giant green jungle.

She was right. The garden wasn't getting bigger. THEY were getting smaller!
![the family shrunk tiny, standing under giant blades of grass like a huge green jungle](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-2-illo-1.png)

In a few seconds, the whole family was as small as a ladybird. The lawn was now a giant jungle. A daisy was as big as a tree.

"This is amazing!" said Lili, spinning in a circle.

"This is a DISASTER," said Leo. "We're the size of crumbs!"

Then the ground went *thud... thud... THUD.* Something huge and spiky was coming.

"RUN!" yelled Dad.

But it was only Pip the hedgehog, now the size of a mountain, sniffing at them.
![Pip](/art/illustrations/pip.png)

"It's just Pip!" laughed Lili. She gave his giant nose a pat. "Hello, big fella."

"Okay, that's a bit better," said Mum. "But how do we get big again before bedtime?"

## Scene 3 — Blaze Has a Plan
> Draw this: Blaze the parrot, now huge, leaning down with one wing out like a ramp; the tiny family looking up at him.

A bright shadow swooped overhead. It was Blaze the parrot, and he had heard everything.
![Blaze](/art/illustrations/blaze.png)

"Squawk! I know that mushroom!" said Blaze. "To get big again, you need three drops of dew from the Silver Flower. It grows by the garden tap, far across the lawn."

"Far?" said Leo. "It's right there."

"It's right there for a BIG bird," said Blaze. "For teeny tiny you, it's a giant adventure."
![a huge bright parrot leaning down to a teeny-tiny family on the grass, pointing one wing across an enormous lawn toward a far-away dripping garden tap](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-3-illo-1.png)

Lili stood up tall — well, as tall as a ladybird can. "Then let's go on a quest," she said. "Team, follow me!"

"Did she just say *quest*?" Leo groaned. But he followed. They all did.

## Scene 4 — The Snail Bus
> Draw this: the family riding on the shell of a big slow snail, holding on and laughing, sliding across the grass jungle.

The grass jungle was too tall to walk through. But a friendly snail was sliding by, slow and shiny.

"Hop on!" called Lili. "It's a snail bus!"

So the whole family climbed onto the snail's smooth shell and held on tight.
![the family riding on the back of a giant friendly snail across the grass, holding on and laughing](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-4-illo-1.png)

The snail slid along, slow and wobbly and slimy.

"This is the slowest bus in the world," said Leo.

"But the comfiest," said Dad, leaning back. "Anyone bring snacks?"

"Dad!" everyone said at once. And they all giggled, sliding across the garden on their slippery, sliding bus.

## Scene 5 — The Grumpy Frog
> Draw this: a huge green frog with a wide mouth eyeing the tiny family hungrily; Lili holding up her glowing hands.

The snail stopped. Right ahead sat a frog as big as a bus, and he was looking at them like lunch.

"Well, well," said the frog, licking his lips. "Tiny snacks. My favourite."

"We are NOT snacks!" said Lili. "We're a family on a quest!"
![Lili](/art/illustrations/lily.png)

The frog opened his enormous mouth. But Lili remembered her warm energy power — the one that only wakes up when her family is close. She felt them all behind her, and her hands began to glow.

"Everybody — be loud!" she cried.

So they jumped and waved and shouted all together. Pip rolled up and Blaze flapped his huge wings — *WHOOSH!*
![Blaze](/art/illustrations/blaze.png)

The frog got such a fright that he hiccupped, went cross-eyed, and hopped away. "Too noisy!" he croaked. "I'll find quieter snacks!"
![a giant green frog going cross-eyed and hopping away in fright while a tiny brave girl with glowing hands and her family jump, wave and shout together](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-5-illo-1.png)

## Scene 6 — A Tiny New Friend
> Draw this: a smiling ladybird with a little leaf, helping the family up a steep flowerpot; warm and friendly.

Just past the frog stood a steep, slippery flowerpot. The Silver Flower was right on top — but how could tiny people ever climb it?

"Need a lift?" said a kind voice. It was a ladybird, about the same size as them, with a big friendly smile.
![a smiling friendly ladybird offering a leaf to the tiny family at the bottom of a tall flowerpot](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-6-illo-1.png)

"Yes please!" said Mum. "I'm Mum. This is my whole family."

"Hop on my back," said the ladybird. "I've got six legs and I'm a brilliant climber."

Up and up they went, all holding on, until they reached the very top of the flowerpot.

"Thank you!" said Lili. "Will you stay for the adventure?"

"I'd love to," said the ladybird. "I never had a team before."

## Scene 7 — Three Silver Drops
> Draw this: the tall Silver Flower with sparkly dew on its petals; the family reaching together to catch three glowing drops.

There it was — the Silver Flower, shining bright, with three sparkly drops of dew on its petals.

But the petals were high and wobbly. Reaching the drops was tricky for tiny hands.

"We can do this," said Lili. "But not alone. Together."

So Dad made a strong, steady base. Leo climbed on his shoulders. Mum climbed on Leo's. And Lili climbed right to the top.
![Lili](/art/illustrations/lily.png)

She stretched out her glowing hands and — *plip, plip, plip* — caught all three silver drops.
![a teeny-tiny family making a wobbly tower, a brave girl at the very top reaching out glowing hands to catch three sparkling silver dewdrops on a tall shining silver flower](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-7-illo-1.png)

"One for the grown-ups, one for the kids, one for the pets," said Lili. "Everybody ready? Drink up!"

## Scene 8 — Big Again, Together
> Draw this: the family back to normal size in the sunny garden, laughing, with Pip and Blaze and the little ladybird sitting on Lili's finger.

The silver dew tasted like cold lemonade and sunshine. And then — *POP! POP! POP!* — the whole family grew, and grew, and grew, all the way back to their normal size.
![the family back to their normal size in the sunny garden, laughing together, with Pip, Blaze and a tiny ladybird on Lili's finger](/art/scenes/the-teeny-tiny-garden-quest/scene-8-illo-1.png)

The garden looked small and friendly again. Pip snuffled happily. Blaze did a loop in the sky.

"We did it!" said Lili. The little ladybird sat safe on her finger, no bigger than a freckle.

"You did it, Lili," said Mum, hugging her. "You led the whole quest."

"No," said Lili, smiling at all of them. "WE did it. Teamwork — even teeny tiny."

"Can we have lunch now?" said Dad. "Adventures make me hungry."

"YES," said everyone.

And they all went inside together — the happiest family in the whole garden, big or teeny tiny. THE END.
